# FinCore Reliability Lab v1.0.0

This release presents FinCore as an evidence-backed technology leadership portfolio rather than only an engineering demo.

## Six connected dimensions

- Business and product operations: the customer journey from onboarding, KYC, risk, accounts, and market data through trading and post-trade operations.
- Financial correctness and risk: balanced journals, idempotency, compensation, reconciliation, audit, and explicit failure contracts.
- Architecture and performance: Java 21, Spring Boot, MyBatis, bounded concurrency, Kafka, Outbox, epoch fencing, JVM/GC evidence, and observability.
- People, delivery, and governance: talent, cross-functional decisions, SLO/DR, FinOps, security, vendors, technology radar, and executive communication.
- Practical AI adoption: governed use cases, human baselines, cost and quality gates, kill switches, and 54 controlled coding-agent runs.
- Digital-asset reliability: explicit mappings and boundaries for deposits, reorgs, withdrawals, nonce/UTXO control, signing, and reconciliation.

## Evidence highlights

- 60 authoritative trades produce 60 unique sequences during the market-crash scenario.
- 17 concurrent settlement deliveries produce one financial effect.
- The full customer trading lifecycle passes all eight checks.
- The market-crash recovery scenario passes all ten checks.
- Five machine-readable governance registries are validated by Maven and CI.
